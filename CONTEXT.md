# Nuko Nova Unslop

Nuko Nova Unslop helps people improve prose while preserving its supported meaning, facts, and voice.

## Language

**Mutable prose**: Writing the current request permits the editor to change. Code, quotations, fixed strings, and other protected material are outside that scope unless explicitly included.

**Protected content**: Facts, claims, uncertainty, quotations, identifiers, code, data, and fixed wording whose accuracy or exact form must survive an edit.

**Owned voice**: A voice supported by the author, a supplied sample, an approved brand brief, or the assistant speaking as itself. It never grants permission to invent another person's experience or stance.

**Writing signal**: An observable form that merits editorial review in context. It does not establish authorship or require a rewrite by itself.

**Preservation difference**: A reported change in protected surface content between source and rewrite. Its absence cannot prove that meaning is unchanged.

**Source structure**: Markdown syntax that distinguishes prose from code, containers, comments, tables, and other document material. Recognizing a structure does not itself decide whether its content may be edited.
