# Super Tighten research record

Reviewed October 14, 2026 with Firecrawl and the official ASD Simplified Technical English Maintenance Group sources. No third-party STE skill or dictionary was imported.

## Finding and implementation

ASD-STE100 is a controlled English standard for technical documentation. The current issue is Issue 9, dated January 15, 2025. It has writing rules and a dictionary that constrains meaning, grammatical function, and word forms. Short sentences and ordinary plain language do not establish compliance.

Super Tighten embeds an original combination of this project's human-writing safeguards, its useful-compression method, and STE writing guidance. It covers vocabulary and technical terms, noun groups, verb forms, sentence structure, procedural and descriptive writing, safety instructions, punctuation and counting, and consistent usage. It preserves source truth when a controlled-language rewrite would change a protected claim or fixed text.

Procedural sentences have a 20-word limit and descriptive sentences a 25-word limit under STE counting conventions. Descriptive paragraphs have one topic and at most six sentences. An instruction normally has one action, with an exception for actions that must occur simultaneously. Prerequisites precede the action. The skill distinguishes actual passive actions from participial adjectives and permitted descriptive passive voice with an unknown actor.

STE does not prohibit every spelling ending in `ing`. Its restrictions concern verbal forms, with valid dictionary and technical-noun uses. Nor does it allow all uses of a familiar word: an approved word can have a restricted meaning and part of speech. Company terminology must justify technical nouns and verbs; it cannot provide a blanket exception for ordinary vocabulary.

STE is intended for technical documentation. This owner's combined skill also applies its descriptive rules when explicitly requested for general English prose. That is a product choice, not a claim that ASD recommends STE for all correspondence. Source voice, slang, contractions, figurative language, and complex tense can conflict with controlled English; protect intended meaning and disclose material exceptions.

## Distribution and verification

The official PDF retains ASD copyright and publication restrictions, with special rights for specified organizations. The public package includes independently worded instructions and synthetic examples, not the standard, its dictionary, or its example corpus. The official document remains the authority for full conformance checks. No endorsement or certification is claimed.

The STEMG's public AI guidance warns that plausible language is not verified compliance. Super Tighten can complete a useful rewrite without the dictionary, but must label unverified vocabulary conformance when relevant. Full conformance claims require the current official issue, a review of each word's use, justified technical terms, applicable writing rules, and disclosed protected exceptions. Model output and package tests alone do not supply that proof.
