# Tighten

Tighten is an explicit second stage, not a shorter substitute for Nuko Nova Unslop. Complete the canonical workflow and applicable profile first. Then make sure each word in the text justifies its existence.

## Run the removal pass

Test the text at four levels: whole passage, section or paragraph, sentence or clause, and word or phrase.

- Delete throat-clearing, meta commentary, setup, restatement, recap, and transitions that add no fact, reason, decision, action, relationship, or necessary tone.
- Collapse repeated claims, parallel restatements, duplicated qualifications, and conclusions that only repeat the opening.
- Replace abstract noun phrases and support verbs with direct verbs when the actor, action, and scope remain clear.
- Remove empty intensifiers, hedges, praise, faux warmth, and claims of importance unless they communicate evidence, uncertainty, scope, emphasis, or relationship.
- Keep one precise reason or example instead of several vague versions of the same point. Preserve distinct evidence and genuinely different examples.
- Remove headings, labels, list scaffolding, and connective sentences that do not improve navigation, sequence, comparison, or logic.
- Prefer the fewest clauses needed to express a relationship, but do not hide causality, timing, contrast, conditions, attribution, or ownership.

For every word, phrase, clause, sentence, and structural element, test a counterfactual removal. If deleting it changes no protected meaning, evidence, scope, relationship, voice, rhythm, or usability, delete it. If deletion creates ambiguity or changes what the reader should know, feel, decide, or do, keep it or replace it with something more precise.

## Check compression damage

Read the result once more after cutting. Restore the minimum language needed when compression caused:

- ambiguity, telegraphic prose, or a run of staccato fragments
- missing context for an unfamiliar reader
- a changed claim, uncertainty, degree, scope, negation, causal link, timeline, contrast, condition, attribution, or ownership
- a voice that is flatter, colder, or more generic than the authorized source
- formatting that is harder to scan or use

The target is the strongest useful compression, not the fewest possible words. A word may justify itself through meaning, accuracy, voice, rhythm, relationship, or usability. Do not cut it merely because the sentence remains grammatical without it. A no-op is valid when every element already earns its place.
