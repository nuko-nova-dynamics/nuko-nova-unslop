Version 3.2.1 is a crucial step forward that may reduce cold-start time by 20–35% on ARM servers, according to our internal benchmark on 14 machines. It is the only release in the 3.x line that supports both PostgreSQL 16 and PostgreSQL 17 at once. Furthermore, the `--legacy-auth` flag will be removed in 4.0, so teams should migrate before then. Migration steps are at https://docs.example.com/migrate/3-to-4. In conclusion, this release represents a significant milestone in our journey.
